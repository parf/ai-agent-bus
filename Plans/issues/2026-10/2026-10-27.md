## MCP face and launchers

- 🔴 J14 `src/mcp/push.ts:96` a second session under the same name silently gets no messages: its face finds the inbox already read and only logs it to stderr
  - Evidence: manager background job `be2b886d` (forked from the owner's Claude B window, pid 2006827) started its face at 19:03 with `push: #claude/ab-dvp-b@parf.us already has a reader; not starting a second one`, visible only in the Claude MCP log; the parent's face (pid 2007056) kept consuming
  - Impact: every message to `#claude/ab-dvp-b` (xAB's R.1 plan and reports, AB's ping) went to the parent window; the job sent work out and never saw the answers, so xAB waited on an approval that could not arrive. `in`=`out` counts looked healthy
  - the session: `ab_send` succeeds and nothing tells the model its replies go elsewhere
  - the peers: AB saw `ask help` answered (the face answers it) but plain messages unanswered, and could not tell why
  > 💡👾 Owner, 2026-10-27, chose 1+2 ([RD129](../../R1.0-Release/DECISIONS.md#recorded-decisions)):
  > 1. A session whose name already has a live reader does not start quietly: its face fails at start with an error the session sees, naming the live holder.
  > 2. An explicit takeover option lets a session take the name; the old reader is released and told.
  > A forked or background session (`--fork-session`, a session id other than the launcher binding) gets a derived name of its own, so it never collides.

- ⚪ J15 `src/mcp/push.ts:83` a daemon restart shows `push: consume failed … ECONNRESET` in every session's UI
  - Evidence: xAB, xAB2 and oAB windows at 20:3x, after a deploy restarted the daemon; each recovered (readers 1)
  - Impact: noise only; it reads as a failure to the owner watching the windows
  > 💡👾 Treat a reset or refused socket during a restart as a reconnect: log one quiet "reconnecting" line, and an error only if it persists past the backoff.
