- ✅ J89 The Slack agent dropped an event that arrived before it knew its own Slack identity
  - Symptom: `src/agents/slack/s5.test.ts:146` (the first forwarding test) got 0 copies of 1 under load, twice in gates on 2026-11-02, the second time after the `drain` wait fix, with the whole test taking 11.5 s.
  - Cause: the agent learned its identity (`auth.test`) once at start; a failed or slow first ask was swallowed, and the first event asked once more and, on failure, was dropped with no retry (`forward.ts`, "identity is not known yet").
  - Fix, `1babe117` (0.9.294): the agent asks for its identity before it takes events, with a bounded wait; Slack failures are classified by HTTP status first (429 and any 5xx, and no answer, are try-again; a refusal, a malformed 2xx reply or an unreadable secret are not, so they never hold the start); while it does not know, `status` says `not ready: …` with the reason; a background asker keeps asking on a capped backoff (1 s doubling to 30 s) and restarts when the identity is lost again; an event that still finds the identity unknown is retried three times on the serial pump, then logged at warning with its channel and ts. A bad `SLACK_AGENT_IDENTITY_GIVE_MS` (a test hook) refuses to start. Proof: with a fake `auth.test` failing for 12 s the old code lost the event (red at `:146`) and the new code delivered it; mutants for the no-retry, single-ask, background-loop, restart, classification, backoff and the fail-open setting each went red on an assertion.
  - Gate red on the way: the first version held start-up up to 20 s on any non-identity answer, so about ten `agent_slack` tests whose fake Slack answered without `user_id` (or whose bus could not give the secret) timed out at 10 s; start-up now waits only for try-again answers. The `s1.test.ts` fake gained the `user_id` a real Slack always sends.
  - Sweep: Discord resolves its identity at start and fails closed (the agent does not start); Telegram's single `getMe` is never retried and fails open (no drop), filed as J90. No other bundled agent filters its own posts by an identity.
