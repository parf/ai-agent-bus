## Deploy

- ⚪ J128 One `git-install.sh` run leaves processes and packages on old code: the standalone units and a person's user-runner package copies are not refreshed.
  - Evidence: `#ab-ceo`, 2026-11-07, after the 1.0.54 deploy: `agent-bus-server-info` and `agent-bus-systemd-web` were not restarted, so server-info kept reporting 0.9.303; `tmux-sessions` in `~/.local/share/agent-bus/agent.d` was still the 2026-10-29 copy. Both were fixed by hand.
  - Impact: a deploy reports success while agents run old code, and their reported version is wrong.
