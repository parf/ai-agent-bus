## Gate flakes

- ⚪ 🟡 J108 the agent guide's journal silence check counts live daemon and runner output, so unrelated activity fails a normal gate.
  - Evidence: `src/acceptance/agent-guide/guide.sh:296-297` queries the shared journal tags since `JOURNAL_START` with no fixture or PID filter; the A7 landing's first normal run (`tmp/gate-logs/20261106-002126-ab-w2a_A7.log:3818-3822`, found by `#ab-w2c`) saw two live `#debug@parf.us: debug requested failure 7` lines and failed 1300/1, then passed 1301/0 on rerun at 134 s.
  - Impact: any live bus activity during a gate, such as a check-deploy, can turn a normal gate red; the second rerun-green gate of the night.
  > 💡👾 Attribute journal lines to the fixture (its PIDs or a fixture-only tag) instead of weakening the silence assertion.

- ⚪ 🟡 J111 the gate's lock wait gives up in about a second when the lock is held under its own name, and reports a 30-minute timeout.
  - Evidence: `local/gate.sh:90-92` retries `agent-bus lock … --wait=60s` 30 times, but "you already hold it; use extend" (kind `busy`) returns at once, so all 30 tries fail within a second; F2's landing at 01:02:36 (`tmp/gate-logs/20261106-010236-ab-w2c_F2.log`) failed with "smoke-gate lock not granted in 30m" while `#ab-ceo`'s runner deploy held the lock as `parf`.
  - Impact: any other `parf` holder (a deploy, a hand-run smoke) fails a landing at once instead of queueing it, and the receipt misstates the wait.
  > 💡👾 Bound the wait by elapsed time and sleep between tries when the answer is "already hold"; J79's per-worker lock identity removes the shared-name case.
