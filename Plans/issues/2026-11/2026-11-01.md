## Gate

- ❓ 🟠 J66 the T-19 landing (`d303a43f`, docs only) was red on its first normal run: `go_unit` took 15.115 s against the 15 s fast ceiling
  - Owner: `#ab-lead1` (found by `#ab-w1b`).
  - Evidence: `tmp/gate-logs/20261101-101417-T-19.log`: line 146 `FAST OVER 15s: go_unit`, line 1045 `passed 27, failed 1`, line 1046 `== rerun after rc=1`; the rerun passed 28/0 (`go_unit` 9.154 s) and landed.
  - Cause: not a defect in the change; the default Go build cache was empty when the run began. All 2746 files of `~/.cache/go-build` are dated 2026-11-01 10:xx and its directory mtime is 10:14:17, the second the gate started; the previous landing (14:40 the day before) left no surviving entry. Nothing in `src/smoke*.sh`, `local/gate.sh` or the acceptance scripts cleans that directory, so the clearing came from outside the repository (not identified). The `smoke-gate` lock was held only by the gate itself, and no peer was running a gate then.
  - Measure (own worktree, same six packages and test filter as `go_unit`, one slow lock): warm cache 0.38 s and 1.27 s; empty `GOCACHE` 12.3 s and 12.0 s; a `VERSION` change alone 0.9 s, so a version bump is not the cause. An empty cache costs 12 s alone and about 15 s beside 27 other shards; the gate's own earlier docs-only runs show 1.2 s warm.
  - Impact: any landing that starts on a cold Go cache goes red once on a fast shard at 15 s, then passes on the rerun by design; the rerun here still took 9.2 s, so the margin stays thin until the cache is warm. Reds like this are noise in the gate record.
  - Proposed fix: before the timed run, `local/gate.sh` warms the cache with a build-only pass of the Go packages the fast shards use (outside the timed window). Do not raise the ceiling: [the fast rule](../../../CLAUDE.md#verification) says to narrow, never to enlarge. That change is to the gate script, not to a test, so it is left to its owner.
  - Settled by: `#ab-lead1`: whether the gate warms the cache or a green rerun is accepted as designed
