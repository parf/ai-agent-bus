## Gate

- ❓ 🟠 J66 the T-19 landing (`d303a43f`, docs only) was red on its first normal run: `go_unit` went over the 15 s fast ceiling (15.115 s wall)
  - Owner: `#ab-lead1` (found by `#ab-w1b`).
  - Evidence: `tmp/gate-logs/20261101-101417-T-19.log`: line 146 `FAST OVER 15s: go_unit` (the message carries no elapsed time; the 15.115 s is the shard wall table, lines 1035 and 1038), line 1045 `passed 27, failed 1`, line 1046 `== rerun after rc=1`; the rerun passed 28/0 (`go_unit` FAST_TIMING 9.094 s at line 1198, wall 9.154 s at line 2081) and landed.
  - Cause: not a defect in the change; the default Go build cache was cold when the run began. The first inspection found 2746 files dated 2026-11-01 10:xx and a directory mtime of 10:14:17, when the gate started. That timestamp dates cache reinitialization, not a proven deletion at that second. The scheduled 04:05 cleanup is the leading explanation; see J66b below. The `smoke-gate` lock was held only by the gate itself, and no peer was running a gate then.
  - Measure (own worktree, same six packages and test filter as `go_unit`, one slow lock): warm cache 0.38 s and 1.27 s; empty `GOCACHE` 12.3 s and 12.0 s; a `VERSION` change alone 0.9 s, so a version bump is not the cause. An empty cache costs 12 s alone and about 15 s beside 27 other shards; the gate's own earlier docs-only runs show 1.2 s warm.
  - Impact: any landing that starts on a cold Go cache goes red once on a fast shard at 15 s, then passes on the rerun by design; the rerun here still took 9.1 s, so the margin stays thin until the cache is warm. Reds like this are noise in the gate record.
  - Proposed fix: before the timed run, `local/gate.sh` warms the cache outside the timed window with `go test -run '^$'` over the packages the fast shards test, so the test binaries are compiled and linked too (a plain `go build` would leave most of the cold cost). Do not raise the ceiling: [the fast rule](../../../CLAUDE.md#verification) says to narrow, never to enlarge. That change is to the gate script, not to a test, so it is left to its owner.
  - Settled by: `#ab-lead1`: whether the gate warms the cache or a green rerun is accepted as designed
