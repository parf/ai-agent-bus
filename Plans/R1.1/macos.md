# macOS sandbox

📌 **TL;DR:** MAC.3 moved from R1 to R1.1 on 2026-11-01 (Q299); it remains deferred until the owner asks.

### MAC.3 sandbox

Deferred ([RD166](../R1.0-Release/DECISIONS.md#recorded-decisions)). When the owner asks, the macOS backend (the system's own sandbox profile mechanism) goes behind the existing sandbox port; its acceptance then mirrors the Linux profiles, with a mutant that lets a denied access through.
