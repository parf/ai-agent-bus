# TODO 0.6.0 — record kinds

📌 **TL;DR:** `kind` becomes a closed set of five, `mode` retires into it, a service is an external one that is not on the bus, and 📡 carries secrets in a field of their own.

## Objective

Close `kind` so the daemon answers what a record is instead of a page guessing
it. The design was settled with the owner in discussion; this file holds the
unfinished work and its acceptance. Substance moves to the owning `docs/`
section as each row lands, and decision names to
[decisions](../../docs/decisions.md#settled).

There is no compatibility obligation before 1.1, so **no migration is written**:
the seven records on the live node are registered again with their kind.

## The enum

| Wire value | Glyph | What it is | Write side | Receive side |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `user` | 👤 | a person's queue | who may send | fixed: the person |
| `agent` | 👾 | an agent's queue | who may send | fixed: the agent |
| `queue` | 📮 | registered queue | who may send | a read list |
| `pubsub` | 📣 | pub/sub channel | who may publish | `Subs` |
| `service` | ⟶ 📡 | external, not on this bus | — | — nothing is delivered |

Wire spellings are the lowercase forms already used by `topic` and `agent`;
`service` is fixed by the owner. Say the word and any of the other four change
before J.1 lands — afterwards it is a wire edit.

`generic` and `topic` disappear. ⚙️ stops meaning a record and stays with the
daemon (`agent-busd`, `docs/user/`).

## Remaining work

| ID | Deliverable | Depends on | Acceptance and mutation |
|---|---|---|---|
| J.1 | `kind` is a closed set | — | `register --kind banana` is refused, naming the five. Each of the five registers. Drop the validation and the `banana` check fails; accept an unknown kind only at restore and the register check still fails |
| J.2 | `mode` retires | J.1 | `topic create --kind pubsub` is gone; 📮 and 📣 are registered by kind alone. No stored record carries `mode`, and the field leaves the wire. Leave `mode` populated on a 📣 and a check that the field is absent from `ls -j` fails |
| J.3 | `register` defaults to `service`, a script is an `agent` | J.1 | `register x@h --addr a --protocol p` with no `--kind` stores `service`. `agent-bus start` stores `agent` (`start.go:80`), and the started script still serves its inbox. Revert either literal separately and its own check fails — the two are independent edits, not one |
| J.4 | 📡 requires an address | J.1, J.3 | `register x@h` bare is refused; with `--addr` and `--protocol` it succeeds. Editing an existing 📡 to clear `protocol` is refused, not silently accepted — the check lives where `validatePersonal` lives, on the final record. Remove the edit-path check and the register check still passes while the edit check fails |
| J.5 | `--personal` is `agent` + flag | J.1 | A personal record is `agent`; `Personal` on any other kind is refused. Allow on a personal agent admits only other agents, and refuses a user, a group, `@owner` and `*`. The redundant user-identity rule is gone and a personal 👤 is still refused — by the kind rule. Restore the old `"generic"` literal and the personal-agent check fails |
| J.6 | 📡 holds secrets | J.1, J.4 | A **new field, not [Config](../../docs/03-services-and-topics.md#configuring-a-template)**: shell `KEY=value` text on a `service`, where Config is JSON on a template and stays readable by its own record alone. A secret is returned to whoever the record's ACL admits and refused to everyone else, with no second list; it appears in no listing, no record answer, no page and no log. Remove the read check and the refusal check fails; return the bytes from `Public()` and the listing check fails; store a secret on any kind but `service` and that check fails |
| J.7 | Faces say what the enum says | J.1–J.5 | An agent's record is labelled `👾`, not `📥 Inbox`, and stays with the channels. Channels lists 👤 👾 📮 📣 and Services lists 📡 alone. `/personal` groups channels. `/services` carries 📡, and ⚙️ appears on no record. Point any one page at the old partition and that page's check fails |
| J.10 | The target's own user state is asked only where it exists | J.1 | `suspension(to)` asks `active(r.Name)` of a `user` and an `agent` and of nothing else; a `queue`, a `pubsub` and a `service` are sent to without consulting a user state they cannot have. Owner suspension is unchanged and still applies to all five. Ask it of a `queue` and that check fails |
| J.8 | Docs own the new words | J.1–J.7, J.10 | [services and topics](../../docs/03-services-and-topics.md#how-to-call-it) splits: calling over the bus belongs to 👾, registering an external service to 📡. Glossary, [discovery](../../docs/05-discovery.md) and [glyphs](web/glyphs.md) name five kinds and no `generic`. Decision rows link the owning section and do not restate it |
